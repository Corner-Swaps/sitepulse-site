Privacy Policy — JobsiteHQ

Last updated: October 13, 2026

JobsiteHQ is a field-operations app for construction teams, available as a web app, Android app, and iPhone app. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. JobsiteHQ is operated from British Columbia, Canada.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, phone number, role (owner, office staff, worker), and sign-in credentials.
  • Work records — timecards and shift records (clock-in and clock-out times, hours, overtime), site photos you upload, daily logs, safety records, blueprints, invoices, and expense claims entered in the app.
  • Precise location — GPS position used to verify clock-ins at jobsites, show crew on a live map, and track fleet vehicles. Location is collected only while you are clocked in or using fleet features, and only after the in-app consent described below.
  • Messages — field messages you send in project channels and direct messages.
  • Device and technical data — device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic logs used to keep the service running.

How we use it

Your information is used only to operate the app: scheduling and dispatch, time tracking and payroll support, safety and compliance records, fleet coordination, project documentation, and crew messaging. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

If your employer gave you this app (British Columbia)

Under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), your employer may collect employee personal information that is reasonable for managing the employment relationship, and your employer is responsible for notifying you of that collection and its purposes before it happens. JobsiteHQ processes employee information on the employer's instructions. If you have questions about why your employer collects information through the app, ask your employer first — and you can also contact us at the address below.

Location and GPS consent

The app asks for your explicit consent before collecting live GPS location. Location is used for clock-in verification, crew safety, and dispatch. You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings; withdrawing stops location collection for your account, though features such as the live crew map and dispatch will not work without it. Vehicle GPS devices installed by an employer report while the vehicle is in service; ask your employer about their vehicle tracking notice.

Where your data lives

Data is hosted on Amazon Web Services in Canada (Montreal region). We use encrypted connections, authenticated access, and role-based permissions to protect it. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep it

Work records are kept for as long as your employer needs them for payroll, tax, and employment record-keeping. British Columbia law requires employers to keep payroll records for at least four years, and personal information used to make a decision affecting you is kept for at least one year after that decision. When you ask us to delete your account information, we delete it within 30 days of verifying your request, except where the law requires us or your employer to keep it longer.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below. We respond within 30 days. If your employer administers your account, we may direct your request to them. If a concern is not resolved, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page and in the app, and change the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
