Privacy Policy — SitePulse HQ

Last updated: October 4, 2026

SitePulse HQ is a private field-operations app for construction teams. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, used to sign you in and identify you to your crew.
  • Precise location — used to verify clock-ins at jobsites and to show fleet vehicles on live radar. Location is collected only while you use these features.
  • Photos and videos — site photos you choose to upload to the project gallery.
  • Messages — field messages you send in project channels and direct messages.

How we use it

Your information is used only to operate the app: running projects, tracking time, managing the fleet, sharing site photos, and crew messaging. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is hosted securely on Amazon Web Services.

Your choices

You can ask your site administrator to correct or delete your account information at any time.
